Key results:
1) Large sea spray aerosols (up to 90 μm) were observed frequently in the coastal atmosphere, including during moderate wind and wave conditions.
2) These large aerosols carry intact phytoplankton cells, including small flagellates, diatoms and colonial organisms, providing the first direct airborne observations of this dispersal pathway.
